Organisations Protecting Our Forests

Support Those Making a Difference


Protecting forests requires more than individual action. Conservation organizations work around the world to protect threatened habitats, restore damaged ecosystems, support local communities, and campaign against destructive practices. Supporting these organizations through donations, volunteering, fundraising, or sharing their work can help strengthen efforts to protect our planet.

World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF)

WWF is an international conservation organisation that works to protect wildlife, restore ecosystems, and address threats such as deforestation and climate change. Its work includes promoting responsible forestry, improving the sustainability of agricultural supply chains, and protecting important forest habitats. WWF also works with governments, businesses, and local communities to encourage better environmental practices.

Learn More

Rainforest Alliance

The Rainforest Alliance works with farmers, forest communities, businesses, and other partners to encourage more sustainable land management and agricultural production. Its work includes protecting biodiversity, improving farming practices, and promoting more responsible supply chains. Its certification programs helps consumers and businesses identify certain products produced under sustainability standards.

Learn More

Forest Stewardship Council (FSC)

The Forest Stewardship Council is an international organisation that promotes responsible forest management through standards and certification. Its system helps businesses and consumers identify certain wood, paper, and other forest-based products that meet specified environmental and social requirements. Certification can encourage more responsible management, although it does not mean that every certified product has zero environmental impact.

Learn More

Forest and Bird

Forest & Bird is a New Zealand conservation organisation that works to protect native wildlife, forests, freshwater ecosystems, and other natural environments. It campaigns for stronger environmental protections, supports habitat restoration, and works with communities and volunteers on conservation projects across the country.
