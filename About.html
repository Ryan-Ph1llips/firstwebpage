About this Website

Green Planet is a website created to educate people about deforestation and its impact on our planet. It aims to explain why forests are being destroyed, how deforestation affects wildlife and the environment, and why protecting forests is so important. The website also provides practical ways people can help reduce deforestation and introduces organisations working to protect forests and conserve biodiversity. By raising awareness and sharing information, Green Planet hopes to encourage people to make more environmentally responsible choices and help protect our planet for future generations.
